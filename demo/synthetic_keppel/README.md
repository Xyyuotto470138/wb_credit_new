# Synthetic deal papers: Keppel REIT, new to bank

SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA, not a real record.

Built by `scripts/build_keppel_pack.py`. The borrower and Ocean Financial Centre are real and are described from the saved public pack in `demo/public_pack/keppel-reit/`. Everything in `deal_folder/` is fictional: the facilities, the bank papers, the valuer, insurer, tenants, rents and projections. No real lending relationship is implied. The fictional valuation adopts the publicly disclosed value at 31 December 2025.

`late_update/` holds papers that arrive after the ES is drafted, for the new information scenario: a revised CAS and term sheet (TL-01 raised from SGD 600 million to SGD 650 million), revised projections on the higher loan, and a revised valuation of SGD 2,750.0 million (was SGD 2,880.0 million). They are fictional too. No published figure of the real trust is changed. Load them only after the first draft: scan the deal folder first.
